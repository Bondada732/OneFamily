This is a strong concept: a private “family operating system” that blends money, memories, planning, and secure records under one trusted family account. I’d position it as FamoryAI — one home for your family’s life and future.

Useful additions:

Family roles and permissions: Family Head, spouse/co-admin, adult member, child/limited access, and viewer. Give fine-grained access per feature, document, and financial account.
Shared calendar: Birthdays, anniversaries, school events, bill due dates, renewals, doctor visits, trips, and religious/family occasions. AI can create reminders early enough to act.
Goals and planning: House purchase, education, wedding, emergency fund, retirement, and holidays—with shared contributions, progress tracking, and “are we on track?” guidance.
Budgeting features: Monthly household budget, category limits, recurring payments, subscriptions, cash expenses, split expenses, and receipt scanning.
Investment dashboard: Track mutual funds, stocks, fixed deposits, gold, insurance, loans, and SIPs; show family-level net worth and upcoming maturity/renewal dates.
Emergency vault: Securely store emergency contacts, medical details, blood group, allergies, prescriptions, insurance numbers, nominee information, and a “what to do in an emergency” family checklist.
Document intelligence: Expiry alerts for Aadhaar/PAN, passports, licenses, insurance, vehicle documents, warranties, and school records. Search documents by name or content.
Memory features: Shared albums, trip timelines, voice notes from grandparents, family tree, annual “family yearbook,” and private memory access settings.
Family tasks: Grocery list, chores, household maintenance, medicine reminders, and delegation to specific members.
AI insights: Spending warnings, unusual-transaction alerts, savings opportunities, missed bill reminders, goal projections, travel packing lists, and occasion-gift suggestions.
Privacy and safety: End-to-end encryption for documents, biometric/PIN lock, two-factor authentication, activity history, device/session controls, export/delete data options, and an emergency access/recovery process.
Indian household focus: UPI/manual expense entry, INR support, Aadhaar/PAN document categories, SIP/FD/insurance reminders, multi-language interface, and WhatsApp-friendly reminder sharing.
For a first version, I’d focus on just five pillars: family access control, expenses/budgeting, secure documents, calendar/reminders, and memories. Add investments and advanced AI after people trust the app with their core family information.

One small naming thought: “FamoryAI” is distinctive, but people may initially read it as a typo of “Family.” If intentional, pair it with a clear tagline such as: “FamoryAI — Your family, organized.”
